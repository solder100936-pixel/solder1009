## 工具回傳文字（原樣）
The search turned up useful background on the shortage and EPSDT coverage, but nothing that directly addresses community outings or school transportation.

**Coverage basis:** Private duty nursing is a Medicaid benefit for medically complex children, including those on ventilators, and it falls under EPSDT. One advocacy piece describes the coverage as a federal entitlement for children ([1-1](https://rochesterbeacon.com/2026/03/18/new-yorks-medically-fragile-children-are-staying-in-hospitals-and-long-term-care-facilities-beyond-when-they-should-be-albany-can-fix-it/)). A North Dakota testimony notes that PDN is in section 1905(a)(8) of the Social Security Act and is therefore included in the EPSDT mandate ([9-1](https://ndlegis.gov/assembly/69-2025/testimony/SAPPHUM-1012-20250326-43775-N-TERZAGHI_DAMON.pdf)).

**Shortage and unfilled hours:**
- A Florida case involved a judge ordering the state Medicaid program to provide 90 percent of authorized PDN hours to children. The state argued that meeting this in the middle of a nursing shortage was not feasible for roughly 2,750 children ([18-2, 18-5](https://www.wusf.org/courts-law/2023-07-24/citing-nursing-shortage-state-seeks-stay-of-judges-order-on-kids-with-complex-conditions)).
- Mississippi reporting attributes the shortage partly to travel and contract jobs drawing nurses away, and to low Medicaid hourly rates ([7-1](https://mississippitoday.org/2022/10/05/medicaid-pay-increase-could-shore-up-in-home-nurses/)).
- North Carolina's Medicaid rate for PDN is cited at $45 an hour, with advocates calling low pay the biggest barrier to recruiting nurses ([13-3, 13-4](https://www.cityviewnc.com/?p=5646)).
- A 2025 California bill would require the state to compare authorized EPSDT PDN hours with hours actually delivered, and to assess whether rates are sufficient to fill authorized hours ([15-9, 15-10](https://legiscan.com/CA/text/AB2240/2025)).

**Gaps:** I did not find sources on whether Medicaid PDN covers nurse accompaniment on community outings or school transportation. Those questions would need a targeted search of state Medicaid manuals or EPSDT guidance, or of state fair-hearing decisions.
