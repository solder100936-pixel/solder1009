I ran one search, on the Blue Badge half of your query. It didn't cover Motability, so I haven't checked that part.

**Blue Badge for children under 3:** Councils generally accept a child under 3 whose condition means they always need bulky medical equipment that is hard to carry. Ventilators are the standard example. Croydon's guidance lists them with suction machines, feed pumps, parenteral equipment, syringe drivers, oxygen administration equipment and similar items. A second route covers children who must be kept near a vehicle for their health, and Croydon's fact sheet describes that category as well (one or both categories may apply). Councils usually want medical evidence. North Northamptonshire asks for medical evidence from a hospital or GP. The badge runs out the day after the child's third birthday, and a new application is needed after that.

Criteria and paperwork differ by council, so the local authority's own guidance is the one to follow.
