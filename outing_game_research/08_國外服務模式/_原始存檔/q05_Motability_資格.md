## 工具回傳文字（原樣）
The search confirmed the core eligibility rule but didn't cover advance payments or waiting times for wheelchair-accessible vehicles (WAVs).

- **Who can join:** Eligibility rests on the mobility component. Most sources say the enhanced rate of PIP's mobility component qualifies you, and standard or lower rates don't. Higher Rate Mobility Component of Disability Living Allowance (DLA) is also a qualifying benefit, but that is the older DLA benefit, not PIP. Scottish equivalents, the enhanced mobility rate of Adult Disability Payment and the higher rate of Child Disability Payment, also qualify, per Scottish residents in receipt of the enhanced rate mobility part of Adult Disability Payment.
- **Remaining award length:** One source says at least 12 months remaining on the award is generally required, so a shorter award may mean waiting.
- **Advance payment:** One source notes that many models are available at £0 Advance Payment. That is a single retailer-style claim and I did not find a general rule for advance payments.
- **WAVs:** One source says the enhanced PIP mobility rate lets you lease a mobility scooter, powerchair, car or WAV through the Motability scheme. I found nothing on WAV waiting times.
- **Upcoming changes:** Sources say the scheme is changing. One reports that the reforms are set to take place from 1 July 2026 and will affect vehicle choice, mileage limits, and eligibility for some claimants. Check current GOV.UK or Motability guidance before relying on this.
- **Reliability:** One result (a .gov.ng domain) gave a PIP enhanced rate of £64.50, which conflicts with the £80 from April 2026 cited elsewhere. I'd discount that source.
