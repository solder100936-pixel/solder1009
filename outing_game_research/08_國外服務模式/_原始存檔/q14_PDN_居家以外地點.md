## 工具回傳文字（原樣）
The federal private duty nursing rule lists three locations, but the "normal life activities" language you're asking about doesn't appear in it, and I found no 2016 federal rule that adds it.

- **42 CFR 440.80 locations:** The current text lists (1) His or her own home; (2) A hospital; or (3) A skilled nursing facility. The 1998 edition shows the same three locations, offered at the option of the State. The regulation's amendment history shows no 2016 change.
- **"Normal life activities" language:** It appears in state rules, not the federal private duty nursing regulation. New Hampshire's home health statute provides Medicaid coverage at their residences and in any non-institutional setting in which normal life activities take place. Colorado's private duty nursing rule says the service is provided in the home, or outside of the home when normal life activities take the client away from the home.
- **2016 rule not confirmed:** The federal HCBS settings rule I found is dated January 2014 (CMS-2249-F/2296-F), not 2016. It establishes a more outcome-oriented definition of home and community-based settings, rather than one based solely on a setting's location. I found no source tying it to 440.80 or to the phrase "any setting in which normal life activities take place."

If you need the federal phrase, check the Federal Register for a 2016 Medicaid home health or private duty nursing rule directly, since my search didn't surface one. The state-level language above is the closest match I found.
