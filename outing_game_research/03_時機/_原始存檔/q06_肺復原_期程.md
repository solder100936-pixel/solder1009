- **Duration threshold:** A SEPAR (Spanish respiratory society) recommendation document sets the floor at 8 weeks or 20 sessions. It states that rehabilitation programs should last at least 8 weeks or 20 sessions. A separate slide deck attributed to ATS/ERS guidance gives a different figure, a minimum of 20 sessions, at least three times per week., and also a minimum program length of 6 weeks. I couldn't confirm that slide deck's attribution, so I'd check the original ATS/ERS statement before relying on it.
- **Durability of benefits:** A narrative review reports that the benefits of pulmonary rehabilitation diminish over 12–24 months following programme completion. That is longer than the 6–12 month window in your query. The same review says there are no strong recommendations for repeating a PR programme. The SEPAR document also notes that the best strategy for maintaining the benefits of PR in the long term is unknown.
- **Fewer sessions:** A real-world conference abstract (ERJ 2023) found that 8 home-based sessions matched 16 in outcomes, and it suggests that when personalized less than 10 sessions may be enough. This is a single retrospective analysis, so it doesn't overturn the guideline threshold.
