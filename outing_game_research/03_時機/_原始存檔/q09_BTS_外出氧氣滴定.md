## 工具回傳文字（原樣）
I searched once. The results don't include the full text of the BTS 2015 ambulatory oxygen protocol, so I couldn't confirm its exact titration steps.
- **Target saturation:** One trial registry summary says the flow rate should keep SpO2 between 90% and 94%, and attributes this to BTS. It also says there is no consensus on oxygen dosage during activity (BTS-guideline).
- **Practice walk:** The BTS appendix on the AOT assessment protocol says a practice walk test should be performed and without one the improvement in walking distance from air to oxygen is likely to be overestimated.
- **Flow increase on exertion:** A 2011 Portuguese letter and its authors' reply discuss titration. The reply says that according to the COPD ATS Guidelines it is recommended that the resting flow rate be increased by 1l/min during exercise. The same reply adds that there is no standard titration method. This is a secondary source citing the ATS guideline, not the BTS text.
- **Titration walks:** The letter describes a model with a walk at baseline, a walk to titrate oxygen flow, and a walk to evaluate response. It is a commentary, not a BTS recommendation.
- **Follow-up:** An Irish nursing flashcard says the BTS 2015 and ATS 2020 guidelines provide additional details on LTOT and AOT. A quality-standard summary citing BTS 2015 says patients should be referred and undergo appropriate formal assessment for consideration for AOT provision including an exercise test.
To verify the exact BTS 2015 titration steps, check the BTS Home Oxygen Guideline Appendix 1 (AOT assessment protocol) directly.
