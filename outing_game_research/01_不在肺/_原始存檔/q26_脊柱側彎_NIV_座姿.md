## 工具回傳文字（原樣）
The search returned mostly clinical studies on home NIV in kyphoscoliosis, with little on infection triggers, seating specifics, or travel.

- **Home NIV outcomes:** A 2020 European Respiratory Journal abstract followed home NIV patients with kyphoscoliosis from 2000 to 2016. It reported good long-term survival, with lower BMI and fewer NIV days linked to reduced survival. The use of home NIV has good long term survival for patient with kyphoscoliosis and CHRF. Lower BMI, fewer days of NIV usage predicted reduced survival.
- **Acute episodes:** A study of 18 patients hospitalised for acute respiratory failure found NIV worked in roughly 75% of kyphoscoliotic patients. It tends to fail in patients with more severe illnesses, and the study also looked at mouthpiece ventilation added to nocturnal NIV.
- **Chronic failure treatment:** A 2003 ERJ paper compared home oxygen with home ventilation. Its authors noted that when kyphoscoliosis is complicated by cardiorespiratory failure, the prognosis is grim. However, improved survival is possible with appropriate treatment.
- **Infection risk:** A case report of a patient with kyphoscoliosis and bronchiectasis describes radiological evidence of bronchiectasis with recurrent episodes of infection, and recommends multidisciplinary management. A seating review notes that bronchiectasis can develop in people with severe skeletal deformity and recurrent infection.
- **Seating and posture:** A physiological review of seating states that in the case of severe kyphoscoliosis profound haemodynamic changes may occur, and that seating design should weigh these effects against other needs.
- **Rehabilitation:** A 2015 Respiratory Care study of 23 patients on home oxygen and NIV found a short pulmonary rehabilitation programme was part of multidisciplinary management. Two of the 23 were excluded for being wheelchair-bound, so it doesn't directly address wheelchair users.

**Gaps:** Nothing in these results covers travel, hypercapnia-specific infection protocols, or evidence-based wheelchair seating prescriptions for this group. Those would need separate searches.
