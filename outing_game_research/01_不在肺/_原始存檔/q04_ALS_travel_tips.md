## 工具回傳文字（原樣）
The search turned up a few useful pieces, though little of it comes directly from ALS Association travel guidance on ventilators or eye-gaze devices.

**Equipment and backups:** The ALS Canada fact sheet suggests packing a backup battery for the BiPAP or wheelchair, cough assist and suction, a communication device, a low-tech backup such as an alphabet board, and spare clothing. Charging everything fully before leaving home covers the BiPAP, wheelchair, external battery, suction, cough assist, and communication device.

**Planning and energy:** The ALS Association's holiday travel post advises being conscious of your energy level and building in enough time for breaks to rest. It also suggests a wheelchair rather than a walker during travel so that energy is saved for planned activities (if you use a walker, you might want to use a wheelchair instead while you are traveling. This can help you save your energy).

**Air travel:** The fact sheet says almost everything you can do to improve your flight experience takes place at the planning stage. A forum thread points to an online resource titled "Air Travel With a Ventilator," which is worth checking for detail on flying with a BiPAP or ventilator.

**Eye-gaze and BiPAP:** This is a real limitation. A university capstone notes that BiPAP masks currently limit ALS patients' access to eye-tracking communication devices, due to the occlusion of the mask over the eyes. Users should test their setup before travel and consider backup options like the alphabet board, and may want to ask their equipment provider or local assistive technology program about alternatives.

**Other resources:** The ALS Association has a webinar, "Smooth Journeys: Essential Tips for Traveling with ALS," and a blog post, "Helpful Tips for Traveling With ALS," that cover transportation and planning. I could not access their full text, so I can't confirm their specific ventilator or eye-gaze recommendations. The Association's blog also points to TSA's section on traveling with medical devices, which is useful for security screening.
