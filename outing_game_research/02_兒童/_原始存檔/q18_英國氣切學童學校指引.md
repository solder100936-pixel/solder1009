## 工具回傳文字（原樣）
I didn't find the DfE's statutory "Supporting pupils with medical conditions" guidance itself in these results. The results are mostly local council and NHS documents, plus older COVID-era coverage.

- **Long-term ventilation is handled separately.** An East Sussex guidance document on tracheostomies in education says it does not cover children who also need long-term ventilation (This guidance does not apply to children who also require long term ventilation). A Nottinghamshire NHS training page adds that children under the Long-Term Ventilation Team need additional training from that team or another provider (additional training should be sought from them or another provider).
- **Training for school staff is child-specific.** In the East Sussex model, the community children's nursing team trains and assesses up to three school staff, and training new staff should only be undertaken in exceptional circumstances. The Nottinghamshire course says that once staff pass, they are competent to care ONLY for that child they have been trained on.
- **Suctioning can be delegated, with conditions.** A Lancashire school document lists tracheostomy care, including suctioning, as a task that may be delegated to non-health staff. It is based on Royal College of Nursing guidance and requires a child-specific risk assessment and training, and demonstrate that they possess the necessary level of competency and confidence. The list also says the schools must liaise with health professionals about which procedures can be delegated (Schools must liaise with the relevant health care professional regarding which of the procedures outlined below can/cannot be delegated to staff.).
- **Infection control.** Earlier COVID-era DfE guidance treated suctioning as an aerosol-generating procedure, which caused problems for some children's return to school. Later guidance clarified that oral or nasal suction is not classed as an AGP. Check whether this guidance is still current before relying on it.

For the authoritative position, look for the DfE's current "Supporting pupils at school with medical conditions" statutory guidance and your local integrated care board's long-term ventilation team protocols.
