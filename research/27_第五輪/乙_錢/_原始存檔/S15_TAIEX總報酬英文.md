I couldn't find a clean year-by-year table of TAIEX Total Return Index year-end closes for 2003 through 2010. The results do point to a few partial sources:

- **Index basics:** The total return series commenced January 1, 2003, and the data is provided since 2003/1/1 on the Taiwan Stock Exchange site. Its base date is 2002.12.31, with a base value of 100.
- **Annual returns in an SEC fund filing:** A 2011 filing lists TAIEX Total Return Index figures in a row without clear column headers, so I can't reliably map them to specific years. Its annualized figures (One year · (2.5) · Three years % pa · 24.0 as of October 31, 2011) are for periods ending in 2011, not calendar years 2003–2010.
- **Rolling 12-month figures:** A later fund filing gives rolling 12 month performance for periods ending January 2015 and January 2012, such as 36.5% for the 12 months to January 2011. These are not calendar-year returns.
- **Year-end data exists but wasn't visible:** The Taiwan Stock Exchange Fact Book includes a TAIEX Year-End Performance (2000-2009) table, and a 2011 edition has one for 2001–2010. These appear to cover the price index rather than the total return index, and the search results didn't show the numbers.

To get the exact figures, I'd check the TWSE's TAIEX Total Return Index page or the year-end performance tables in the Fact Book. The index's historical data on taiwanindex.com.tw may also work, though I didn't confirm it has a year-by-year breakdown.
