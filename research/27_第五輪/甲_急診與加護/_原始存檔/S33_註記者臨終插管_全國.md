## 回傳文字（原樣）
I didn't find a study that matches your exact combination of criteria: a nationwide analysis of decedents with a registered advance directive shown on the national health insurance card, with intubation and CPR rates in the last month of life. The closest work is partial matches.

- **DNR letter of intent and the insurance card:** A Taiwanese study of 754 DNR letter-of-intent orders found that most were backed by scanned or paper documents, and about 14% were also noted on the insurance card. Of the 754 DNR-LOI orders, 646 were supported with scanned files or paper documents (85.68%), 106 (14.06%) with a note on the NHI card. This appears to be a hospital-based sample, not a nationwide one, and it is not clear it measures intubation or CPR in the final month.
- **Legal mechanism:** Taiwan's Hospice Palliative Care Act lets a "letter of intent" be noted on the national health insurance certificate, with the file stored in a central database. Article 4 establishes a mechanism called the "letter of intent", and the competent authority will note the letter's existence in the individual's national health insurance certificate.
- **Advance directive prevalence:** A 2022 nationwide study used records from 18 randomly selected hospitals to estimate how many terminally ill patients had advance directives before the Patient Right to Autonomy Act. It is a retrospective review of medical death records, but the snippets I retrieved don't give the proportions or the last-month intubation and CPR figures.
- **Life-sustaining treatment near death:** A 2022 nationwide cohort of cancer decedents found that EOL discussions correlated with a lower likelihood of receiving cardiopulmonary resuscitation (AOR = 0.45, 95% CI 0.43–0.47), endotracheal intubation (AOR = 0.92, 95%CI 0.90–0.95). Its window is the last three months of life, not the last month, and it measures discussions rather than registered directives.
- **Dementia versus cancer:** A 2017 study using national insurance claims found that PwD had a higher risk of enteral tube insertion and feeding, endotracheal intubation and tracheostomy, mechanical ventilation, hemodialysis and cardiopulmonary resuscitation, measured over the last year of life.

For the exact figures you want, the next step would be a targeted search for the full text of the 2022 DNR letter-of-intent study or a dedicated analysis of the national insurance database.
