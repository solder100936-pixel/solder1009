## 回傳文字（原樣）
The study you're looking for appears to be a 2018 Taiwanese observational study in the *Journal of the Formosan Medical Association*. Its details match your query, though the search results don't show the PubMed record's journal name, so that link is my inference from matching figures.

- **Design and size:** The study enrolled 135 terminally ill patients whose mechanical ventilation was withdrawn at one Taiwanese medical center between 2013 and 2016 (PubMed record, 1-3).
- **Outcomes:** About 93% died, and 102 patients (75.6%) died within one day of withdrawal. The median survival was 45 minutes (DOAJ record, 2-3).
- **Premedication:** Adherence to the premedication guideline was weak. Full adherence was 17.8%, and no adherence was 42.2% (PubMed record, 1-6).
- **Authors and title:** Yu-Shin Hung and colleagues, "Clinical characteristics and survival outcomes of terminally ill patients undergoing withdrawal of mechanical ventilation" (DOAJ record, 2-1).

The study uses the term "withdrawal of mechanical ventilation" rather than "compassionate extubation." Related Taiwanese work from Kuang Tien General Hospital, published in *Clinical Interventions in Aging* in 2015, reports median death times of about 0.3 hours after extubation in cardiac-arrest patients and 97 hours in patients with advanced cancer (DOAJ/Dove record, 5-6).
