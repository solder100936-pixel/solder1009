## 回傳文字（原樣）
The search didn't turn up a Taiwan emergency-department figure for NIV failure or intubation rates in elderly COPD patients. The closest results are:

- **A registered prevalence study (NCT06213623)** says failure is usually defined as needing intubation, which doesn't fit older patients who have do-not-intubate decisions. So it measures "absence of NIV success" at one hour instead. This suggests Taiwan-specific rates may be hard to compare with other studies.
- **A northern Taiwan single-center study (2019–2021)** looked at NIV weaning in admitted AECOPD patients, not ED failure rates. Its weaning-failure definition counts repeated NIV, intubation, high-flow oxygen, or long-term NIV support.
- **A National Taiwan University Hospital record** describes a 2006–2008 US emergency department sample, not Taiwanese data. It found NIV use ranged widely between hospitals, with a median of 11%.
- **A Dutch ED study (van Gemert et al.)** examined ED factors linked to switching from NIV to invasive ventilation in COPD. It reports mortality up to 30% after NIV failure, but it isn't from Taiwan.

For Taiwan-specific figures, the next step would be to search PubMed or the Taiwanese journals directly, using terms like "Taiwan," "acute hypercapnic respiratory failure," and "emergency department."
