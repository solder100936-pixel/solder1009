## 回傳文字（原樣）
I didn't find a Taiwan single-center retrospective study that reports an HFNC failure rate specifically in elderly pneumonia patients using ROX. The closest matches are:

- **Taiwan, COVID-19 cohort:** A retrospective study of COVID-19 patients with hypoxemic respiratory failure in Taiwan found that the ROX index was higher in the HFNC success group than in the failure group, and that a falling ROX during HFNC use could give early warning of failure. The retrospective study enrolled patients in Taiwan. Early intubation may be considered in patients with a higher VICE score or a lower ROX index.
- **Taiwan, multicenter:** A 2025 J Clin Med study of acute respiratory failure patients included 107 received HFNC before intubation and 461 received conventional oxygen therapy across 14 hospitals in Northern Taiwan. It found that pre-intubation HFNC failure did not significantly affect hospital mortality. This is multicenter, not single-center, and the cohort was COVID-19.
- **Single-center, not Taiwan:** A 388-patient single-center retrospective study of acute hypoxemic respiratory failure reported a failure group of 132 (about 34%) versus 256 successes, with a mean age of about 68. The primary endpoint was the need for escalation of respiratory support to tracheal intubation. The source does not identify the country in the results I saw, and I could not confirm it is Taiwanese, so treat it cautiously.
- **ED study, Korea:** A single-center retrospective ED study found that among the 97 included patients, 25 (25.8%) failed HFNC therapy in the ED. The ROX index had an AUC of 0.709 before HFNC started. The authors appear to be Korean (Yonsei), not Taiwanese.

For the specific Taiwan elderly-pneumonia single-center figure, I'd suggest searching PubMed or Taiwanese journals (e.g., Chang Gung or National Taiwan University hospital series) directly.
