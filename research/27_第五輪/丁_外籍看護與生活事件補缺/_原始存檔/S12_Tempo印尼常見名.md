## 回傳文字（原樣）
The Tempo article lists the top 10 female names as Nurhayati, Sulastri, Sumiati, Sri Wahyuni, Sumarni, Sunarti, Siti Aminah, Aminah, Ernawati, and Kartini. Fakrulloh shared the 20 most popular names for males and females in Indonesia, posted under his social media handle. The article is dated 2 February 2023, so the list is about three years old.

A separate Databoks report on 2024 e-KTP data, based on Dukcapil figures, names Sutrisno as the most common male name recorded on e-KTPs in 2024. That report is paywalled, so I couldn't check its female rankings.
