## 工具回傳文字（原樣）
**NSW Home Ventilation Program (EnableNSW)**

- The program funds adults and children who depend on mechanical ventilation and have significant care needs, so they can leave hospital and be cared for at home. The Home Ventilation Program (HVP) provides funding assistance for adults and children who are dependent on mechanical ventilation and have significant care needs to leave hospital and be safely cared for at home.
- Providers must be accredited for complex care, including ventilation and tracheostomy management. EnableNSW only contracts accredited care providers, i.e. one who is accredited to provide care for a person with complex care needs, including management of your ventilation and your tracheostomy.
- The EnableNSW eligibility indicator is only a guide. It does not consider your individual circumstances. Eligibility for EnableNSW programs generally requires residence in NSW or Lord Howe Island, and that you aren't already getting assistive technology through another government program. To be eligible for assistance from EnableNSW, applicants must be residing in NSW or Lord Howe Island, and not be eligible to receive assistive technology through any other government-funded program.
- Home ventilation equipment itself falls under the Home Respiratory Program, which lists ventilator circuits, tracheostomy, and continuous ventilation among its items. Continuous ventilation, heated humidifiers, ventilator circuits and accessories, and tracheostomy are listed under the Home Respiratory Program. The continuous ventilation form also notes that a new application form is required every two years OR if the person's circumstances change.
- I did not find any source stating that this equipment is provided free of charge, so I can't confirm the "free" part of your query.

**Victorian Respiratory Support Service (VRSS)**

- VRSS is a statewide service for adults with chronic respiratory failure who need a ventilator at home, and it is based at Austin Health. The VRSS is a state-wide specialist program providing a range of services to adults with chronic respiratory failure who require treatment with a ventilator at home. The VRSS is based at Austin Health, Heidelberg Campus.
- Its home outreach covers tracheostomy care, ventilator and mask support, and discharge education. The VRSS Outreach Service provides discharge education, ongoing phone support, home visits for mask or ventilator issues, and care for tracheostomy tubes.
- Referrals come from doctors. Referrals are made to the service by general practitioners and specialist physicians. The current process is to download the VRSS referral form and return it by email or fax to the service. Download and complete the VRSS Referral Form. Return the referral to us via email or fax.
- A 2011 article describes the funding model, under which VRSS supplies the ventilator and related equipment and provides annual home visits. This is older information and may have changed. VRSS supplies the ventilator and associated equipment, provides annual home visits for equipment maintenance, mask fitting and other equipment issues.

**Key difference:** NSW's program runs through EnableNSW eligibility and accredited providers. VRSS is a specialist clinical service that refers and supplies equipment directly. The sources don't establish whether either program is free, so check directly with EnableNSW (1800 362 253) or VRSS (03 9496 3688) before relying on cost.
