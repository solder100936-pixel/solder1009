## 工具回傳文字（原樣）
I only ran one search, covering the Carer Payment work limit. I didn't search for the aged care respite or Carer Gateway questions, so I have nothing on those yet.

**Carer Payment work limit:** The old 25-hour weekly cap has been replaced. Services Australia says the change took effect 20 March 2025 and lets recipients work up to 100 hours in a 4-week period ([source](https://servicesaustralia.gov.au/examples-working-while-getting-carer-payment)). Recipients still must provide care for a significant part of each day. Some older DSS and Services Australia pages still describe the 25-hour rule, so check which version applies to your situation.

**Respite days:** Carer Payment includes 63 temporary cessation of care days per calendar year ([source](https://servicesaustralia.gov.au/changes-to-work-rules-if-you-get-carer-payment)). Respite days can cover hours above the 100-hour limit, with one day covering up to 8 extra hours ([source](https://servicesaustralia.gov.au/examples-working-while-getting-carer-payment)).

**Suspension instead of cancellation:** If someone goes over the limit without enough respite days, or earns too much employment income, Services Australia may suspend payment for up to 6 months rather than cancel it ([source](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/sites/default/files/2025-05/working-while-you-get-carer-payment-transcript.docx)).
