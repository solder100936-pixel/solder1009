## 工具回傳文字（原樣）
I ran one search. It covered the funding route well, but not the specifics of 24-hour carers or family training for tracheostomy or home ventilation.

**Funding route:**
- In England, adults eligible for NHS Continuing Healthcare have had a legal right to a personal health budget since October 2014 (Adults who are eligible for NHS Continuing Healthcare (CHC) funding have had a legal right to have a personal health budget since October 2014.).
- For people at home, NHS England expects a personal health budget by default (people who are eligible for NHS Continuing Healthcare and in receipt of care at home should expect to have a personal health budget as the default NHS Continuing Healthcare operating model.).
- The MND Association says that if you qualify for CHC and live at home, the funding is provided as a personal health budget (this will be provided in the form of a personal health budget.). Its guidance also notes that this is unlikely in the early stages of the disease. Even in the later stages, it can be difficult to get.
- Budgets can be taken as direct payments, which the MND Association describes as receiving your budget in direct payments to your bank account.
- A personal health budget is only available in England (it only applies in England.). In Wales, the petition source says this is not available in Wales.

**Eligibility and barriers:**
- The MND Association says people with motor neurone disease (MND) often face delays, denials, and appeals when applying for NHS Continuing Healthcare (CHC).
- Its CHC factsheet notes that if you are living at home and you receive Attendance Allowance (AA), Personal Independence Payment (PIP) or Disability Living Allowance (DLA) and receive NHS Continuing Healthcare, your benefits will not be affected.

**Ventilation and home care:**
- Non-invasive ventilation is a common part of home respiratory support for MND (Non-invasive ventilation (NIV) equipment helps manage breathing difficulties.). I did not find NHS guidance on tracheostomy or invasive ventilation training for family carers, or on 24-hour staffing under CHC.

For the specifics you asked about, the MND Association's CHC factsheet (10D) and the NHS England personal health budget FAQs are the best next sources. Your local ICB or CHC case manager can confirm what a particular package would cover.
