## 工具回傳文字（原樣）
The search confirms the core structure but not the full set of figures you listed.

- **Start date and budgets:** The government's classifications document says the quarterly budgets and annual amounts took effect 1 November 2025 and are indexed each July. Budgets are reviewed annually.
- **Eight classifications:** Sources agree there are eight ongoing-service classifications, with higher classifications for higher care needs. Each has a quarterly budget, and the annual budget is split into quarters. Separate budgets apply to transitioned Home Care Package participants.
- **Dollar amounts:** One provider's table gives approximate quarterly budgets of about $2,750 (Level 1), $4,000 (2), $5,500 (3), $7,500 (4) and $10,000 (5). A government fee schedule lists the same figures for Levels 1 to 4. I found no reliable figures for Levels 6 to 8, so please check the official schedule for those.
- **Participant contribution:** An AGS fact sheet lists standard rates for a full pensioner at the minimum threshold as 0% for clinical care, 5% for independence, and 17.5% for everyday living. The 50% and 80% rates you mentioned did not appear in the results. They may apply to other income and asset tiers, but I could not confirm that.
- **Unspent funds:** A government fact sheet says participants can carry over up to $1,000 or 10% of the quarterly budget, whichever is more.

Recommended next step: check the official health.gov.au classifications document and the MyAgeCare fee estimator for the full eight-tier budget table and the complete contribution rate schedule.
