## 工具回傳文字（原樣）
The official position is that the 2026/27 limits are unchanged, and the cap reform is no longer just delayed.

- **Limits confirmed:** The GOV.UK local authority circular for 2026 to 2027, published 17 February 2026, keeps the lower capital limit at £14,250 and the upper at £23,250. A person with assets above the upper capital limit is responsible for the full cost of their care in a care home.
- **Cap status:** The £86,000 lifetime cap and the higher means test have not taken effect. One source says the cap was originally set for October 2023, delayed to 2025, and subsequently indefinitely postponed. Another reports that the government scrapped its predecessor's plan, which would have raised the upper threshold to £100,000. Treat the calculator site (the source of the "indefinitely postponed" wording) as less authoritative than the GOV.UK circular.
- **Outdated pages:** Some council pages still describe a 2025 start date, such as Southwark's note that the implementation of the charging reform has been delayed until October 2025. Those dates no longer apply.
- **Minor discrepancy:** One council agenda gives the upper limit as £23,500, which looks like a typo for £23,250.

I didn't find a current official statement on a new timetable for reform, so check GOV.UK if you need the latest position.
