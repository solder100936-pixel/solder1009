## 工具回傳文字（原樣）
The official figure for 2026 is £92 per LPA, though some sites still show the old £82.

- **Current fee:** The gov.uk LP13 guide, updated 20 April 2026, says applying to register one LPA costs £92. Several other guides say the fee rose from £82 on 17 November 2025, with all applications received on or after 17th November 2025 paying £92.
- **Outdated figures:** Some pages still list £82 or £92 without a date, so check gov.uk before relying on them.
- **Per-LPA cost:** Property and Financial Affairs and Health and Welfare are separate documents, so a typical person pays twice. One guide puts this at £184 per person (or £368 for a couple).
- **Fee reductions:** The OPG offers reduced or zero fees through form LPA120. One guide says two routes to a reduced or zero OPG fee exist, applied for alongside registration, but the results don't name them.
- **Life-sustaining treatment:** The results say the health and welfare form asks about life-sustaining treatment (Health LPA only). They don't explain what the "option A" and "option B" wording is, so I couldn't confirm it. The gov.uk LP1H form itself would be the authoritative source for that.
