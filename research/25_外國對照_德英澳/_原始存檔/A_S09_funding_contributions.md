I ran one search. It supports the general-taxation point but does not confirm the 50% and 80% contribution figures.

**Funding is from general taxation, not insurance.** One guide says the Australian Government provides the majority of funding through general taxation revenue, while care recipients contribute based on their financial means. Another puts the Commonwealth share at approximately 75% of all aged care costs. These are third-party sites, and the 75% figure varies by source. The search did not return an official Department of Health figure.

**Contributions are means-tested.** For Support at Home, an income assessment is conducted. Participants get a budget for the services they need, and that budget depends on assessed care needs and includes government funding and the participant's contributions. Contributions can change over time, and lifetime caps apply to Support at Home contributions.

**The 50% and 80% figures are unconfirmed.** None of the results mention a 50% contribution for independence or an 80% contribution for everyday living, or any pensioner or self-funded retiree split. The closest match was a change to home care contributions on 1 November 2025, which the results do not describe in detail. Please check the official My Aged Care or Department of Health pages before relying on those percentages.
