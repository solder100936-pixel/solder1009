## 工具回傳文字（原樣）
The search gave me a clear answer on the 7-day registration rule, but not on the 6-month doctor-attendance test.

**Registering the death (7 days):** NSW's service portal says all deaths that occur in NSW need to be registered with the Registry of Births, Deaths and Marriages within 7 days of the burial or cremation. The portal also notes that usually the funeral director completes the registration, but a next of kin or relative can also register the death. The required items include a medical certificate Cause of Death (issued by a doctor).

**Reportable death (Coroners Act 2009):** The Act defines a reportable death in section 6, and its list starts with a person's death is a reportable death if the death occurs in any of the following circumstances, including violent or unnatural deaths and sudden deaths of unknown cause. Section 38 bars a doctor from certifying the cause of death when the death is reportable. The table of contents shows that section, but the search did not return its text. I could not confirm the specific "not attended by a doctor within 6 months" wording, so please check the full text of section 6 on the NSW legislation site before relying on that timeframe.
