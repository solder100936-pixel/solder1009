The search confirmed the 2026 contribution rates but returned nothing on Hilfe zur Pflege or the Sozialamt, so that part of your query is unanswered.

**2026 rates:** Sources agree the general rate stays at 3.6 %. One reports it has held steady since the turn of the year and remains unchanged in calendar year 2026. Childless members aged 23 and over pay a 0.6 % surcharge, bringing their total to 4.2 % (the surcharge applies to everyone from age 23, and the full contribution then comes to 4.2 % for 2026). The reduction for parents of two to five children under 25 is 0.25 points per child (the reductions apply from the second through fifth child under 25, with 0.25 points deducted per child from the general rate).

**Planned changes (sources conflict):** A cabinet-approved reform would raise the childless surcharge to 0.9 points from 1 January 2027, giving 4.5 % in total (the surcharge rises by 0.3 points from 0.6 to 0.9 percent on 1 January 2027, bringing the total to 4.5 percent). A May 2026 report instead described a proposal of only 0.7 points (a 0.1-point increase to 0.7 percent), and a separate article cites 0.7 as well. Treat the 0.9 figure as the cabinet position and the 0.7 figure as an earlier proposal; I did not verify the final legislative status.
