## 工具回傳文字（原樣）
I ran one search. The results confirm the overall structure of the 1 November 2025 changes, but they don't support the $130,000 lifetime cap in your query, and I couldn't find a 2026 retention or basic daily fee figure.

- **Who it applies to:** The new arrangements cover people who first enter permanent residential care on or after 1 November 2025 (These may apply to you if you first enter residential care on or after 1 November 2025.). People already in permanent care on or before 31 October 2025 keep their existing fees unless they opt in (Their fee arrangements stay the same from 1 November 2025 unless they choose to opt in to the new arrangements.).
- **Ongoing fees:** The new means-tested fees are the hotelling contribution and the non-clinical care contribution (Hotelling contribution; Non-clinical care contribution). A basic daily fee also still applies under the new arrangements (a basic daily fee and a hotelling contribution).
- **Accommodation:** The accommodation arrangements expand from two to three options (From 1 November 2025, this will increase to 3 different accommodation arrangements.). Under the new rules, the payment types include a refundable accommodation contribution, where eligible, and a daily accommodation contribution (a refundable accommodation contribution (RAC), if you are eligible for assistance; a daily accommodation contribution (DAC), if you are eligible for assistance).
- **Caps and thresholds:** The official guidance says the lifetime cap and the hotelling supplement and income and asset thresholds are indexed in March and September, with current figures published in the Schedule of Fees and Charges (The lifetime cap amount is indexed in March and September each year and will be available in the Schedule of Fees and Charges for Residential Care from 1 November 2025.). A third-party blog gives a non-clinical care contribution daily cap of $107.32 and a pre-November lifetime cap of $86,185.23, but I couldn't verify those against an official source (Non-Clinical Care Contribution (NCCC) daily cap: $107.32 per day). Nothing I found mentions a $130,000 lifetime cap, so treat that figure as unverified.
- **Refund and retention terms:** I found nothing on the 2026 retention rules or the basic daily fee amount. The January 2026 Department of Health fact sheet and the Schedule of Fees and Charges would be the primary sources to check for those.
