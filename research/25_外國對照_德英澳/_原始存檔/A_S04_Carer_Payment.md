## 工具回傳文字（原樣）
I ran one search. The results agree on the broad picture, but the exact September 2026 figures come mostly from third-party calculators, and I couldn't find anything on the Commonwealth Home Support Programme.

**Carer Payment (September 2026):** Several sites give $1,237.70 a fortnight for a single carer and $933.00 each for a couple from 20 September 2026, and say these include the Pension Supplement and Energy Supplement — the same rates as the Age Pension. The official Services Australia page in the results only says that the Department of Social Services adjusts these rates every 20 March and 20 September. I could not confirm the $1,237.70 figure against an official source.

**Carer Allowance:** Most sources give $162.60 a fortnight. One says rates effective 1 January 2026, while another says next indexation: 20 September 2026. So it's unclear whether a September 2026 change applies. The income limit is consistent across sources: family adjusted taxable income cap combined, you + partner, per year < $250,000. Note that one source (the topleftdesigns page) has a 2026 Carer Allowance figure that matches the others but is otherwise unreliable.

**Respite:** One source says Carer Payment includes respite entitlement 63 days/year. That is a single third-party figure and should be checked on Services Australia.

**Commonwealth Home Support Programme:** None of the results mention it, so I have no information on it.

**Recommendation:** Check the current rates directly on Services Australia's Carer Payment and Carer Allowance pages before relying on these figures.
