## 工具回傳文字（原樣）
**Summary:** Most sources agree on the 2026/27 figures, with Carers UK the most authoritative result. I found no authoritative source on the overpayment review, so treat that part as unverified.

- **Weekly rate:** Carers UK gives £86.45 per week (2026/27), up from £83.30 in 2025/26.
- **Earnings limit:** Carers UK puts it at £204 per week (after deductions) from 6 April 2026. Several other sites also give £204. One aggregator gives £207 (New Weekly Earnings Limit (2026/2027): £207), and another gives a range of £204 to £207 (index 12-7). I'd treat £204 as the figure to use, since it has the strongest support.
- **Hours requirement:** Multiple results list the 35-hour caring requirement (e.g., index 13-4), which is consistent with the long-standing rule.
- **Overpayment review:** Only low-quality aggregator and content-farm pages mention an overpayment review, a "landmark review," or a debt write-off. One says exceeding this limit, even by £1, means the entire benefit is lost, but that is a general rule rather than a review finding. None of these pages cite an official DWP publication, so I could not confirm the review's scope, timing, or outcome. Check gov.uk or a DWP announcement before relying on it.
