## 工具回傳文字（原樣）
The search turned up UK guidance on home ventilation, but nothing specific on how NHS Continuing Healthcare funds a 24-hour package for a tracheostomy-ventilated adult.

- **Service model:** The NHS England service specification describes home ventilation as respiratory support at home, usually by mask but also via tracheostomy. Patients who need assisted ventilation for 14 or more hours in 24 are at risk of serious harm if it is interrupted. The same document says such patients and their carers need round-the-clock nursing and technical support, and that back-up ventilators and consumables must be available (back-up ventilators and consumables are required).
- **Referral pathway:** The document also says patients should move to a complex centre when parts of their care fall outside what a standard home ventilation unit can provide.
- **BTS guidance:** The British Thoracic Society's Model of Care for Complex Home Mechanical Ventilation sets out standards for complex services, including patient pathways, follow-up and outreach. It covers tracheostomy-ventilated patients and ongoing weaning at home. Its appendices include sample documents such as a ventilation care plan, a tracheostomy passport and carer training standards (Appendix 11: Care training standards; Appendix 12: Ventilation care plan; Appendix 13: Patient tracheostomy passport).
- **Provider example:** The Royal Brompton's outreach ventilation and tracheostomy team says it supports over 250 patients at home or in care facilities, and it runs a rapid response service for long-term ventilated patients who become unwell in the community.
- **Continuing Healthcare:** One private provider says it works with NHS Continuing Healthcare teams within Integrated Care Boards to set up care packages. That is a commercial source, so treat it as anecdotal.

**Gaps:** The results don't cover CHC eligibility criteria, how the 24-hour care is funded, or the NHS payment scheme details. The NSW Home Ventilation Program results are Australian, so they don't apply to the UK. A follow-up search on "NHS Continuing Healthcare eligibility home ventilation" or the BTS guidance's funding section would be the next step.
