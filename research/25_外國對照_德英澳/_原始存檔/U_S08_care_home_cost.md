## 工具回傳文字（原樣）
The search returned 2026 figures for self-funders, but nothing specific on deferred payment agreements.

**Weekly self-funder costs (UK averages, 2026)**
- **Residential care:** carehome.co.uk gives about £1,298 per week, roughly £5,192 a month, or about £67,496 a year (If you are paying for your own care: The average weekly cost is £1,298. The average monthly cost is £5,192. The average annual cost is £67,496.)
- **Nursing care:** the same site gives about £1,535 per week (If you are paying for the nursing home yourself: The average weekly cost is £1,535 per week.). Lottie puts it lower, at about £1,512 per week (the average cost of self-funded nursing care is £1,512 per week and £6,552 per month.).
- **Dementia care:** carehome.co.uk lists about £1,343 per week for residential dementia care, and Lottie lists about £1,375.

**Regional variation**
London is the most expensive. One London guide gives London average (Self-funded nursing care): £1,759 per week. Northern regions and Wales are cheaper; carehome.co.uk's Wales figure for residential care is about £1,156 per week (Wales £1,156£1,394£1,217£1,440).

**NHS-funded nursing care offset**
For nursing homes, the NHS contributes a flat weekly amount. The weekly rates for NHS funded nursing care in England for 2026/2027 are: Standard rate – £267.68. Higher rate – £368.24. This reduces the net cost for self-funders with nursing needs.

**Deferred payment agreements**
None of the results gave details on deferred payment agreements. One guide (retirementexpert.pages.dev) says it covers the deferred payment scheme, but its content was not in the results, so I can't cite its details.

**Caveats**
- Figures differ between sources by roughly £20–£40 per week, so check the source date.
- Several results came from calculator or aggregator sites (such as caretoolkit.pages.dev, treatcompare.com and a site with an unusual domain) that I could not verify. I'd rely on carehome.co.uk, Lottie and official GOV.UK or DHSC material for the figures.
- For deferred payment agreements, check GOV.UK or your local council's adult social care pages directly.
