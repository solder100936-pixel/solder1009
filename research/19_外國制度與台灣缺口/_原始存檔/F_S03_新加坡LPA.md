## 工具回傳文字（原樣）
The search turned up the answer to the certificate issuer question, though the fee details conflict across sources.

**Who can certify:** The MSF page says the LPA Form will have to be witnessed and certified by an LPA certificate issuer. Eligible issuers are a medical practitioner accredited by the Public Guardian, a practising lawyer, or a registered psychiatrist. MSF also publishes a list of most visited LPA Certificate Issuers.

**Fees:** The certificate issuer is paid separately from the OPG application fee. One March 2026 report says LPA Form 1 applications will be made free of charge for all Singapore citizens from April 1, and applicants must also pay a separate certification fee to a Certificate Issuer. TheSmartLocal estimates that issuer fees typically start from $24.

**Conflicting information:** An AnnabellePsychology page, last updated December 2023, lists the Form 1 fee as $0 until 31 March 2026 and $75 after that. A PropertyNet page gives the 2026 Form 1 application fee as $90 for permanent residents and $230 for foreigners, which I could not confirm elsewhere. Check the OPG portal (publicguardian.gov.sg) for current figures before applying.
