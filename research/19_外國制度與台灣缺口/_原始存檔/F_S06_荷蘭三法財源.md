## 工具回傳文字（原樣）
The search returned solid material on the three-pillar structure and how each is funded, but nothing specific on mantelzorg (informal family care).

**Structure and funding**
- **Wlz:** Covers round-the-clock care. One source describes it as covering intensive 24-hour care for people with chronic illness or severe disability, funded through a national insurance premium. Another puts the premium at 9.65% levied on income in the first tax bracket, deducted by employers as part of wage tax.
- **Wmo:** Covers lighter municipal support. Sources describe it as tax-funded municipal block grants, with municipalities responsible for all other forms of long-term care since January 2015.
- **Zvw:** Covers health insurance, including home nursing. Eligibility depends on being a resident, paying the income-related contribution, and paying the nominal premium.

**Conflicting sources on Wlz funding:** One source says long-term care in the Netherlands is funded by general income tax. Others describe a premium instead. The premium-based description is more consistent with the rest of the results, but the sources don't settle the question. Check the official Belastingdienst or Zorginstituut pages before relying on either figure, especially the 9.65% rate, which I couldn't confirm for 2026.

**Mantelzorg:** None of the results covered informal care or how it relates to these schemes, so I can't say anything reliable about it from this search.
