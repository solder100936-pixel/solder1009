## 工具回傳文字（原樣）
Facilitator-led ACP appears to be free or low-cost, though sources differ on the details.

- **Conflicting cost information:** A HealthXchange page says there is no payment for advance care planning. A LifeSG FAQ, however, notes that some service providers may charge a nominal fee for doing an ACP when you book with a facilitator, and that cost may vary, so please check with the respective organisations for details.
- **Free self-service option:** The digital tool myACP lets Singaporeans easily make their Advance Care Plans online for free, without the need to see a facilitator.
- **Finding a facilitator:** AIC says ACP facilitators are found in all the government restructured hospitals, polyclinics and community nodes. AIC also provides a map to find a facilitator, and you can book directly with them.
- **Contact:** AIC's contact line is 1800 650 6060 (per the AIC ACP brochure).
- **Living Matters background:** The national programme was started in 2011 and is run under the Agency for Integrated Care.

I didn't find a published fee schedule, so for exact pricing, check with the specific provider before booking.
