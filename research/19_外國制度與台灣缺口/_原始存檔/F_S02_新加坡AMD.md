## 工具回傳文字（原樣）
The official line is that there's no government fee for the directive itself. The main cost is the doctor's consultation for witnessing it, and that varies by clinic.

- **Government cost:** The AMD form itself is free. It's voluntary, free of any government fee to register.
- **Doctor's fee:** As an AMD must be made through a doctor, you may have to pay a fee to the doctor for this service. One guide puts a private GP's fee often in the region of $30 to $80; a polyclinic visit is cheaper for citizens and permanent residents.
- **Private option:** Raffles Medical lists an Advance Medical Directive (AMD) Review $59.08 (inclusive of GST), which includes witnessing by its doctors.
- **Second witness:** The second witness must be at least 21 years old or above, and can be the doctor's nurse or any other suitable person. Neither witness can have a vested interest, such as being a beneficiary under your will or insurance policy.
- **Where to get the form:** You can pick it up from polyclinics, hospitals and many GP clinics, or download it from the Ministry of Health.
- **Conflicting detail:** One procedure site says the processing time for this procedure is one month and that a fee is paid as per the authority's request. This doesn't match the official and other guide statements, so treat it with caution and check with the Registrar of Advance Medical Directives.
