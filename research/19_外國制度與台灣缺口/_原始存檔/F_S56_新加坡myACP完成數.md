## 工具回傳文字（原樣）
The search turned up the launch details and two completion counts, but no official figure specific to myACP.

- **Launch and eligibility:** MOH launched myACP on 19 July 2025. With myACP, generally healthy Singaporeans can now go online to self-document their care preferences. It is free and needs no facilitator visit. It is open to those aged 21 and above with no existing serious illness, such as cancer or dementia.
- **Completion numbers:** Healthcare IT News reported that as of July 2025, 77,000 advance care plans have been completed in the country. A personal finance site says more than 87,000 Singaporeans have now completed an Advance Care Plan, with 94% aged 50 and above. That site doesn't cite a source or date, so treat it as less reliable.
- **Gaps:** I found no official myACP-only count, and no 2026 MOH update. A parliamentary question from 15 October 2025 asked for totals and breakdowns by age, gender and race, but the answer wasn't in the results. The Healthcare IT News target of "51% by 2027" is unclear and may be garbled, so I wouldn't rely on it without checking the original source.
