Some important things to note

If you have a serious condition

The list of serious conditions, or illnesses, currently include:
Cancer
Chronic neurological diseases
Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD) and End-stage Lung Disease
Dementia
End-stage Renal Failure (ESRF)
Heart Failure
Severe Liver Disease

If you are living with any of the above, you will need to make your ACP with specially trained facilitators who can guide you in making decisions surrounding your condition. To locate these facilitators, click on the 'With serious conditions' button on the map .

Fees

You may have to pay a nominal fee when doing an ACP with a facilitator.

However, there are some community providers who can help you make your ACP for free:
Fei Yue Community Services
SATA CommHealth
Society of Sheng Hong Welfare Services
Montfort Care
Home Nursing Foundation
Sunlove Abode for Intellectually Infirmed Ltd
Lions Befrienders Service Association

Do note that these facilitators are not trained to help those with serious illnesses make their ACP.
